Objections we hear

Every question is a doubt to remove.

My data is sensitive. Where does it go?

It stays on your infrastructure. We deploy LOSC on your servers — or run local Ollama models so nothing leaves your network. We do not store, mirror, or access sensitive data unless you explicitly authorise it for a specific task.

What if you build it and disappear?

You own the source code. The system runs on your infrastructure. The monthly retainer is cancellable — if we vanish, your system keeps working and any Greek developer can pick it up. There is no lock-in by design.

I tried ChatGPT. It hallucinated. Why is this different?

ChatGPT alone is a generalist model with no access to your data. We build retrieval-grounded systems: every answer is anchored to your documents, with citations to the source. When the model doesn't know, the system says so — instead of inventing.

How do I know it works before I commit?

The 48-hour prototype is the commit test. We build a working system on a slice of your real data, free of charge if scope is small. If it's not useful, you walk. If it is, we agree on production scope.

What about GDPR?

Local deployment + Ollama option = your data never leaves the EU, never enters US clouds. We sign a DPA. We are based in Athens, governed by Greek and EU law, advised by L+A.

Do you work with my industry?

Current focus: law firms, medical practices, accounting firms, hotels, real estate, B2B services. If your problem is "we have lots of information and not enough time to use it" — we fit. Discovery call confirms.

What if the model gets better next year?

Your system upgrades with it. We are model-agnostic — Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, local Ollama, future models — all plug into the same architecture. You don't pay for a rebuild when GPT-6 ships.
